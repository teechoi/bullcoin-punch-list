Hero

#the-mark
BlockerB01 · conversion

"Buy $BULL" doesn't buy anything

Both buy buttons — the one in the header and the one in the hero — just scroll down to the "How To Bull" instructions. There is no swap link anywhere on the page. The only route to actually trading is the Dexscreener card near the bottom, four screens down, labelled "Watch the Chart".

This is the whole point of the page and it currently dead-ends into a paragraph telling people to paste a contract address somewhere else.

FixPoint both buy buttons at the live swap with the token pre-filled, opening in a new tab. Keep "How To Bull" as the secondary route for people without a wallet yet — a text link under the button, not the button itself.

header  a "Buy $BULL"    href="/"  → JS sets #how-to-bull
hero    a "Buy $BULL 🚀"  href="/"  → JS sets #how-to-bull
hero    a "Join the herd" href="/"  → JS sets #bullish-links
BlockerB04 · brand

The hero is the 3D low-poly GIF — that's not the character we use

The first thing anyone sees is bull-main.gif, a faceted 3D render. That is not the canonical Bull. The canon is the flat 2D character: white graphic body, black shades, horns, nose ring, green leaf tail tip — the model that every community asset is built from.

Separately, that GIF is 360×360 natural pixels stretched to 412 CSS px, so it is soft even at the size it's shown.

FixSwap in the canonical model or one of the on-canon action stills below, at 2× the display size. Reference: bull-canon.vercel.app/assets/bull-model.jpg. Retire the 3D GIF from the site entirely.

BlockerB02 · reach

No share image — every X and Telegram post renders a blank card

There is no og:image and no twitter:image. The card is declared as summary_large_image, which means X reserves a big image slot and then shows nothing in it. Telegram gets the same treatment. Also missing: og:url and a canonical link.

X and Telegram are the entire distribution for this token. Right now every link anyone shares looks broken.

FixAdd a 1200×630 share image using the canonical Bull on the lime ground, plus og:image:width/height, og:url and og:site_name. Then test the real URL through X's card validator and a Telegram DM to yourself before launch.

present  og:title, og:description, og:type,
         twitter:card=summary_large_image,
         twitter:title, twitter:description
missing  og:image, og:image:width, og:image:height,
         og:url, twitter:image, link[rel=canonical]
MajorM12 · icons

The favicon is a .webp, and there's nothing else

The only icon declared is /bullcoin/logo.webp. There's no apple-touch-icon, no 180px PNG, no manifest. Anyone who saves the site to a phone home screen, or opens it in Safari, gets a blank tile.

FixShip favicon.ico (32px), icon-192.png, icon-512.png and apple-touch-icon.png (180px) from the head-only version of the Bull mark, and add a small web manifest.

PolishP19 · seo

Set canonical and robots before this hits the real domain

No link[rel=canonical] and no meta[name=robots]. Harmless on a share-token preview, but the moment this ships on the production domain we want a canonical pointing at the apex URL so preview and production deployments don't compete.

FixAdd canonical to the production origin, and keep preview deployments on noindex.

Community carousel

#community
BlockerB03 · broken

Most of the meme wall never loads — you get empty outlined boxes

Six of the nineteen images on the page never decode. They sit in the carousel track with loading="lazy", and because the track is offset horizontally, the browser decides they're off-screen and never fetches them. The files are fine: requested directly, all four return 200 image/webp.

The ones that do load arrive one at a time over roughly ten seconds. First impression of the community section is three empty rectangles.

FixDrop loading="lazy" on the first three slides and eager-load them; preload the rest on carousel mount rather than relying on viewport detection. Set explicit width/height on every slide so the cards don't pop in, and give each one a solid lime placeholder so a slow slide reads as loading, not as missing.

BlockerB05 · content

The image filenames are rotated, so the alt text describes the wrong pictures

The asset filenames don't match their contents — the names are shifted by one position through the set. The site's alt text was written from the filenames, so it now describes pictures that aren't there. Anyone on a screen reader is told about a stadium when they're looking at a coffee cup.

Verified against the same filenames on bull-canon.vercel.app, so this is wrong at the source, not just on this build.

FixRename the files to match their contents (do it in the canon repo too so this doesn't come back), then rewrite each alt to describe the picture actually shown. One line each, plain description, no ticker copy.

MajorM07 · scroll trap

The carousel eats the scroll wheel — the page freezes over it

With the cursor anywhere over the carousel, the mouse wheel does almost nothing to the page. Move the cursor a couple of hundred pixels lower and the same gesture scrolls normally. On a laptop trackpad, mid-page, this reads as the site having hung.

FixOnly capture horizontal wheel intent (deltaX), and let vertical deltaY pass through to the page. If the track uses overscroll-behavior or a wheel handler with preventDefault, scope it to the horizontal axis.

cursor over carousel  (y≈400)  6 wheel ticks →   ~7 px of page scroll
cursor below it       (y≈670)  5 wheel ticks → ~470 px of page scroll
MajorM09 · controls

Carousel arrows are too small, sit on top of the art, and the disabled one looks broken

Both arrows measure 38×38 px — under the 44 px minimum for a thumb. They're positioned over the third card rather than beside the track, so they cover the art. And the "previous" arrow in its disabled state is a dark grey circle on a light ground, which reads as a rendering fault rather than "you're at the start".

There's also no dot row or counter, so in a seven-item set there's no way to know how far through you are or that there's more than one screen of memes.

FixTake the arrows to 44 px, move them outside the track or onto the section header, give the disabled state a low-contrast outline instead of a filled dark circle, and add a dot row or an "n / 7" counter. Make sure left/right arrow keys drive it too.

MajorM10 · layout

The third card is sliced off mid-image at the container edge

The track is wider than its container, so the third meme is cut vertically through the middle of the picture with a hard edge. There's no fade, no partial-card treatment, nothing that says "scroll for more" — it just looks like the layout overflowed.

FixEither fit whole cards to the container width, or make the peek deliberate: show a consistent slice with a gradient mask into the ground colour so it reads as an affordance.

PolishP20 · feedback

Confirm the "Save" and "copy address" buttons say something happened

The Save button only appears on hover, so on touch it's invisible until tapped. And the contract pill copies on click — check that it shows a "Copied" state afterwards, because right now there's no way to tell from the button whether it worked.

FixShow Save persistently on touch devices, and give both buttons a visible confirmed state for a second or two after the action.

Bullnomics

#bullnomics
MajorM06 · trust

The numbers read "0 total supply" and "0% LP burned" until the animation fires

The three stat tiles are count-up animations that start at zero and only run when you scroll them into view. Before that, the page genuinely says 0 total supply, 0 / 0 tax and 0% LP burned. Scroll past quickly and that's what you see. It's also what anyone gets with JavaScript off, with reduced motion on, and what a page snapshot captures.

"0% LP burned" is the exact opposite of the claim we're making, on the one section people read to decide whether to trust the token.

FixPut the final values in the HTML and animate from them, not toward them. When prefers-reduced-motion is set, skip the count entirely and show the numbers.

PolishP13 · copy

Cut "Not Bitcoin, Bullcoin. It's better."

It opens the section by measuring us against something else, which is the weakest way to introduce anything. It's also a straight superiority claim, sitting a screen above a footer that says the token has no intrinsic value and no expectation of return. Pick one.

FixKeep "Numbers so simple even a bear could understand them." and stop there. It's funnier without the second sentence.

The Mark & The Lore

#the-lore
MajorM11 · layout

"The Bull Mark" has a half-empty left column and a stray rule in the card

The left column holds a headline and two lines of text, then about 250 px of empty black before the section ends, while the USPTO card on the right runs full height. The section reads unfinished.

Inside the card, there's a vertical hairline to the right of the Bull logo with nothing after it — looks like an empty second cell in the header grid.

FixFill the left column — the mark itself at large scale, or pull the three headline facts (registration number, date, status) out as big type — and remove the empty grid cell so the divider goes away.

MajorM08 · links

Every nav link is href="/" with the scrolling bolted on in JavaScript

All five header links, all five footer links and both hero buttons have href="/". Clicking works — JavaScript intercepts it, scrolls, and writes the hash — but cmd-click, middle-click and "open in new tab" all just reload the homepage at the top. There are no real section URLs to share, and nothing crawlable.

FixUse the actual fragment in the href (href="#the-lore") and let the JS call preventDefault on top of that. Costs nothing and everything starts working the way people expect.

PolishP14 · copy

"check it for yourself bulltard" is on the one link that's asking to be believed

Calling each other bulltards on X is the culture and it should stay there. But this particular link is the trademark record — it's the moment the page asks a stranger to go verify something. Insulting them in the same sentence undercuts the only piece of hard evidence on the site.

Fix"Check the USPTO record yourself ↗". Keep the in-group language for the meme wall and the marquees, where it earns its place.

PolishP17 · performance

The X embed is a third-party iframe carrying our mission statement

The Lore section's right column is a live X post embed. It takes a few seconds to appear, ships X's tracking with it, and can't be styled to match anything else on the page. The mission copy inside it is already told better by the four numbered points sitting immediately to its left.

FixReplace the iframe with a static styled quote card and a "Read on X ↗" link. Faster, on-brand, and no third-party script in the critical path.

PolishP18 · motion

Every section starts near-invisible and fades in on scroll

Scroll reveals are applied to more or less everything. Scroll at any speed and you pass through sections that haven't faded in yet — during this review, "The Bull Mark", "Bullnomics", "How To Bull" and "Bullish Links" were each blank on arrival for a moment. It also means any snapshot of the page below the fold is empty.

FixKeep reveals for one or two moments that earn them and let the rest of the page render at rest. Whatever stays needs a prefers-reduced-motion branch that shows content immediately.
